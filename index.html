What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds, source links, categories and comparison notes. A useful list records the exact item, selected variant, measurements and costs, helping you compare options before deciding what to buy.

Oopbuy shopping guide

A shortlist in four steps.

Build a useful Oopbuy product list around your actual needs, rather than the number of links you can collect.

01 / DEFINE

Choose a purpose.

Pick one category and a realistic budget. Write down the fit, color and material you want before browsing.

02 / DISCOVER

Collect a few options.

Browse relevant CAFANS categories. Save each exact product link and variant so similar listings stay distinguishable.

03 / COMPARE

Check the evidence.

Compare measurements, descriptions and available photos. Record missing details instead of assuming them.

04 / REVIEW

Confirm before paying.

Recheck the seller, stock, current price, return terms and delivery estimate through the service you intend to use.

The comparison notebook

Good finds deserve good notes.

When comparing Oopbuy finds, keep the same fields for every candidate. A consistent format makes differences in fit, cost and missing information easier to spot.

Estimated total: item price + domestic shipping + service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties. Include currency conversion charges where relevant.

A practical reference for using product lists thoughtfully and checking the information behind a fashion find.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds, source links, categories and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on how accurately each entry records product details.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not provide an official Oopbuy integration or a downloadable spreadsheet.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the exact item and variant, measurements, material information, seller identity, current price, return terms and estimated delivery costs. A photo or a listing title alone does not establish authenticity.

Can a spreadsheet tell me the final delivered price?

A spreadsheet can help estimate a total, but the final amount depends on the item price, domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and any applicable taxes or duties.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the seller's item-specific measurements with a similar item you already own. For shoes, check the model's size chart and foot-length guidance; for clothing, compare chest, shoulder, sleeve, waist and length measurements.

Are the pictured brand items available on CAFANS?

The brand photographs are editorial style references. They do not confirm that a pictured item is listed, available or authentic on CAFANS. Open the relevant category and verify the individual listing.
